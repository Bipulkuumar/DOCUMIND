# DocuMind Enterprise Company Policy

## 1. Refund and Subscription Terms
All software subscription products purchased directly from DocuMind include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the original date of purchase.
To process a refund request, customers must submit a ticket via the support portal at support@documind.ai with their invoice number.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## 2. Paid Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year.

## 3. Remote Work and Security Standards
Employees working remotely must connect to company systems exclusively via encrypted VPN.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employee accounts.
Passphrases must be at least 16 characters long and changed every 90 days.
